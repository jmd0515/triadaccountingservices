Common questions

What accounting software do you use?

QuickBooks Online is our preference and what most clients use. We also work in Intuit Enterprise Suite, NetSuite and Sage Intacct. If you're on something else, ask; we've worked in most of the common platforms.

Do you prepare tax returns?

Our focus is the monthly accounting. We work alongside your tax preparer and hand them a clean, reconciled year-end package. If you don't have a CPA, we can recommend one.

Do I need to be in the Triad?

No. Most of the work is done remotely through your accounting software and a shared folder. If you're nearby and want to meet in person, we're happy to.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Service is month to month with 30 days' notice to cancel. If your books need catch-up work first, that's quoted separately and up front.

What if my volume changes?

Pricing is reviewed with you every six months. If things grow or shrink significantly, we adjust the monthly fee together rather than surprising you with an invoice.

Who has access to my accounts and money?

You stay in control. We work with user access you grant and can revoke anytime, and payments go out only with your approval.
